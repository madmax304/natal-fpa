Natal FP&A — internal QuickBooks Online integration

Natal FP&A is an internal tool and is not available for public connection.

An authorized Natal administrator connects it by running the local authorization command, which opens Intuit’s consent screen and requires signing in as an administrator of the Natal QuickBooks Online company.

Connecting grants read-only access to accounting data. It can be revoked at any time — see Disconnect.

Natal FP&A is an internal tool. It is not offered to, sold to, or usable by the public.
