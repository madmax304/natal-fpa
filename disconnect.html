Disconnect

Natal FP&A — internal QuickBooks Online integration

To disconnect Natal FP&A from a QuickBooks Online company:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps (or Manage your apps).
  3. Find Natal FP&A and choose Disconnect.

Access ends immediately and the stored authorization tokens stop working. Any local copy of the data can be deleted by removing the local database file from the computer running the tool.

Natal FP&A is an internal tool. It is not offered to, sold to, or usable by the public.
