Natal FP&A — internal QuickBooks Online integration

Natal FP&A is a private, internal financial-analysis tool used by Natal to read its own QuickBooks Online accounting data. It is not a commercial product and is not distributed to other organizations.

What it does

It retrieves accounting data — the general ledger, chart of accounts, vendors, customers and standard financial reports — from the single QuickBooks Online company owned by Natal, and stores a copy locally so the finance team can analyze it.

Access

Read-only. The application issues no write requests. It cannot create, modify or delete any record in QuickBooks Online.
